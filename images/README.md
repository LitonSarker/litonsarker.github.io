Add your images here:

- `profile.jpg` — your headshot, referenced by `author.avatar` in `_config.yml`
- `portfolio/*.jpg` — one image per portfolio project, referenced in each `_portfolio/*.md` file's `excerpt` front matter

Your old Google Site images live on Google's CDN (lh3.googleusercontent.com) and can't be pulled in automatically — save them from the live site (right-click > Save Image) and drop them here with matching filenames, or swap in new ones.
