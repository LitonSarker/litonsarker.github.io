**Duration:** 6 months

This project supported the first countrywide 4G network deployment in Bangladesh. I worked on behalf of Ericsson Bangladesh to support Robi Axiata Ltd, developing an automated network analytics & reporting application for effective network performance reporting.

The system autonomously collected network performance raw data, processed it, prepared analytical reports, and emailed them to the relevant personnel — covering 2G, 3G, and the newly deployed 4G network. It helped quickly identify and fix issues arising from the new 4G rollout, and the client found it very helpful in maintaining network quality throughout a smooth, massive nationwide rollout.
